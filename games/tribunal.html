The Tribunal of Utility

You are Jeremy Bentham. The tribunal sits outside time, so your critics come from before and after you: Adam Smith, J.-B. Say, Nassau Senior, Karl Marx, John Stuart Mill, and the historian E. K. Hunt. Each challenge has two parts. First, answer as Bentham would, in his own terms. Then do what the course asks of every argument: state the opponent's point in its strongest form, the form your reply does not answer.
