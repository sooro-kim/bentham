Key Ideas

Ten ideas, opened one at a time

Each idea stays closed until you solve the question in front of it. A wrong answer tells you what it gets wrong, and the next idea opens only when you get this one right.

Quotations from Bentham are from An Introduction to the Principles of Morals and Legislation and from Jeremy Bentham's Economic Writings (ed. W. Stark, 1954), as quoted in Hunt & Lautzenheiser, ch. 6, pp. 125–134. Page numbers in brackets are Hunt's.
