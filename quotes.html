Each sentence is missing its key words. You will not remember them, so reason them out from his theory. Then the full passage and a note open.

Ten passages are quoted as they appear in Hunt & Lautzenheiser, ch. 6, pp. 130–133, with Hunt's ellipses and square brackets; page numbers refer to Hunt, and volume numbers to Jeremy Bentham's Economic Writings, ed. W. Stark (1954), Hunt's source. The axiom from A Fragment on Government and the push-pin sentence from The Rationale of Reward were checked against the Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy and Wikipedia.
